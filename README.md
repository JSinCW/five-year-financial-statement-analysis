# Five-Year Financial Statement Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates a company's financial performance over **five financial years, from 2017–18 through 2021–22**.

The analysis was completed as part of my undergraduate internship project and focused on evaluating the company's liquidity, solvency, profitability, capital structure, and overall financial trends.

## Methods Used

- Comparative Financial Statements
- Common-Size Financial Statements
- Ratio Analysis
- Trend Analysis

## Financial Areas Evaluated

### Liquidity
- Current Ratio
- Quick / Liquid Ratio
- Absolute Liquid Ratio
- Working Capital

### Solvency
- Debt-Equity Ratio
- Proprietary Ratio
- Leverage Ratio

### Profitability
- Net Profit Ratio
- Return on Shareholders' Investment

### Trend Analysis
- Sales
- Expenses
- Net Profit
- Reserves & Surplus

## Key Findings

- Total revenue increased by approximately **71%** between 2018 and 2022.
- Profit for the period increased by approximately **72%**.
- Working capital increased by approximately **92%**, indicating an improvement in short-term liquidity.
- Fixed assets increased by approximately **127%**.
- Long-term borrowings increased substantially during the period.
- The current ratio improved to **2.2** in 2021–22.
- Shareholders' funds represented approximately **52% of total assets** by 2021–22.
- Sales and expenses followed similar upward trends, while net profit and reserves increased steadily.

## Business Interpretation

The analysis indicated that the company maintained generally satisfactory liquidity, solvency, and profitability during the period.

However, the study also identified areas for management attention, including:

- Rising inventory levels
- Increasing operating expenses
- Growth in long-term borrowings
- Declining cash and cash equivalents
- Opportunities to improve capital structure and cost management
